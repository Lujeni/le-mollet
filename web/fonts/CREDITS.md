# Font credits

Served from the binary (`src/web.rs`, `VENDOR`) instead of Google Fonts, so that a ride talks to one origin only.
Files are the woff2 subsets Google Fonts serves (latin, latin-ext), fetched on 2026-10-29. All three are under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
